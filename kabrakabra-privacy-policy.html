KabraKabra Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

KabraKabra ("the app") is a just-for-fun app that estimates how many words you speak each day. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we never see it.

What the app accesses

Microphone. While you have listening switched on, the app uses your microphone to measure when you are talking and when you are quiet (volume and timing). It estimates a word count from that rhythm.

  • The app does not use speech recognition. It never converts your voice to text, so no transcript of anything you say is ever created, stored, or captured.
  • Your voice is analysed in real time on your device. No audio is uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • The Android recording system requires a short-lived temporary file in order to measure volume. The app deletes that file as soon as you tap Stop Listening. If the app is closed or crashes while still listening, the temporary file can remain in the app's private storage (which other apps cannot read) until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • The app only listens while you have it switched on, and it does not record in the background.

What the app stores

The app stores two small things on your device: a daily word-count number for each date (for example, "2026-11-01: 1,240"), used to show your history calendar, and, only if you use the optional "Tune it to your voice" feature, one calibration number (roughly how many beats per word your voice makes). That is all.

  • This data never leaves your device. We do not operate servers that receive it.
  • It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we do not collect any.

Sharing a recap

The Share button creates a picture of your word count and opens your phone's standard share menu. The picture only goes where you choose to send it. We do not receive it.

Children

KabraKabra is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
