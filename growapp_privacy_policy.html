Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes what Here We Are ("the app", "we") collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address (or your Google/Apple account identifier, if you sign in that way), used only to identify your account.
  • Your entries: the gratitude and good-deed text you write each day. This is personal reflection — we store it so the app can render your plant and word clouds, and never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never show your individual entries to any other user.
  • Approximate location (optional): if you opt in to the Public Map, we ask for coarse location only (never your exact/precise location) and immediately reduce it to a ~5km-wide area code. Your exact coordinates are never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere beyond that one-time calculation. Your area is only ever shown on the map blended together with at least 4 other nearby users' areas — never as an individually identifiable pin.
  • Notification preferences: your chosen daily reminder time and device push token, used only to send you the reminders you've asked for.

What we don't collect

We don't collect precise/exact GPS location, contacts, photos, or browsing history, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers.

How your data is used

Solely to run the app: computing your plant's growth and vitality, building your two private word clouds, sending reminders you've opted into, and contributing your area (only in aggregate, only if opted in) to the anonymized Public Map.

Who we share it with

We use Google Firebase / Google Cloud to host the app's data and infrastructure — they act as our data processor and don't use your data for their own purposes. If you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers handle authentication directly; we only receive an account identifier and email, never your password.

Your controls

  • Turn Public Map participation on or off at any time in Settings — this immediately stops your area from contributing to future map updates.
  • Export all of your own entries at any time from Settings.
  • Delete your account at any time from Settings — this permanently removes your entries, plant data, and word clouds, and removes your area from all future Public Map aggregates.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes it promptly and permanently; it isn't recoverable afterward.

Children's privacy

Here We Are is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@hereweare.love
