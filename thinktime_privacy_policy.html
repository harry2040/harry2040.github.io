ThinkTime Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

ThinkTime turns highlights you've already made — in Kindle books, or notes exported from tools like Obsidian — into a shuffled feed worth reopening. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

The short version

ThinkTime has no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you import stays on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere unless you personally tap a Share button — and even then, you choose exactly where it goes.

What ThinkTime collects

Nothing. ThinkTime does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your highlights, notes, or any imported file content
  • Your name, email, or any account information — there is no account
  • Your location, contacts, camera, or microphone — the app never requests these
  • Analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking of any kind

How your data is stored

When you import a Kindle "My Clippings.txt" file, text copied from Amazon's Kindle notebook page, or markdown highlight exports, ThinkTime parses them on your device and saves the result to the app's local storage, on that device only. It never leaves your phone as part of normal use. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and so does the in-app "Clear all highlights" option.

When something does leave your device

ThinkTime has two explicit, user-initiated actions that open your device's native share sheet:

  • Sharing a single highlight — sends that highlight's text to whatever app or person you pick from the share sheet.
  • Sharing your highlights file — bundles everything you've imported into a text file and sends it the same way.

In both cases the share sheet is your device's own operating system feature. ThinkTime hands it plain text or a local file and has no visibility into what happens after you choose a destination.

Opening the Kindle app

Tapping a highlight's "open in Kindle" action uses your device's standard app-linking system to open the Kindle app, or a web search if it isn't installed. No data about this action is sent to us.

Permissions

ThinkTime asks for file access only at the moment you choose to import or export a file, through your device's own file picker. It does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts access.

Children's privacy

ThinkTime does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
